02 Profile & Philosophy · Antoine Reverdy

Precision of hand, memory of fire.

Raised in the hills of northern Rhône and Beaujolais, Antoine Reverdy honed his craft within iconic three-star institutions in Roanne and French-speaking Switzerland before opening his sanctuary in Old Lyon. His North Star: twenty-eight guests at a time, ensuring absolute command over each pan, glaze, and aroma.

“Cooking without chatter. A dish should never present more than three forthright voices: an earthy root, a prime cut, and a meticulously concentrated juice. Everything else is mere distraction. We cook purely on demand, guided by eye, heat, and texture.”

— Antoine Reverdy · Chef & Owner

Mentorship & Masters

Tenures at Maison Troisgros (Roanne) and Crissier. Steeped in the quiet choreography of fine dining and gentle lakeside fish preparations.

Michelin Recognition (2023)

Maison Lumière was founded in 2021. Awarded its first Michelin star within 18 months, celebrated for its restrained poetry and uncompromising local terroir.

Ferme des Roches (Messimy): bitter herbs, heritage root vegetables grown in living soil.
Lake Geneva line-fishermen: arctic char and fera landed at dawn.
Condrieu & Côte-Rôtie estates: direct cellar allocations from biodynamic vintners.
