01 Introduction · Intimate Fine Dining

Clarity and fire, 28 seats in historic Lyon.

One Michelin star, twenty-eight walnut chairs, and an exacting philosophy. Chef Antoine Reverdy distills the storied gastronomy of Lyon down to its purest essence: pristine local ingredients, razor-sharp cooking times, and uncompromising sauces.
